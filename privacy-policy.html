1. Information We Collect

We designed this app to respect your privacy. No account creation, login, email address, phone number, or personal identity registration is required to use the app.

  • On-Device Preferences (Local Storage): Your chosen language, Light/Dark theme, selected weather city, quiz progress, and notification mute preferences are stored locally on your device via browser/WebView localStorage and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Optional Approximate/Live Location (Weather Only): Location access is strictly optional and is only requested if you manually tap "Use My Live GPS Location" inside the Live Weather popup. Your coordinates are sent directly from your device to the Open-Meteo weather API solely to display local temperature and weather conditions, and are never stored on any developer server.
  • Optional Live Quiz Nickname: If you voluntarily join a scheduled Live Quiz session, only the temporary display nickname and quiz score you submit are sent to Firebase Realtime Database for the live leaderboard.

2. Third-Party Services & APIs

To deliver live weather, multi-language support, and performance reliability, the app interacts with the following third-party services:

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com): Provides free live weather and temperature forecasts based on your selected Indian city or optional GPS coordinates.
  • GoatCounter (goatcounter.com): Privacy-friendly, cookie-free aggregate page view statistics that do not track individual users across sites.
  • Google Fonts & Regional Translation: Loads Indian language fonts for crisp typography, and fetches real-time translations when you select one of the extended regional languages online.
  • Firebase Realtime Database (Google): Used only if a user voluntarily participates in the Live Quiz feature.

3. Emergency Helpline Dialer Notice

The Safety & Disaster Survival section displays publicly known national Indian emergency numbers (such as 112, 101, 108, 1930, 181, 1078) for public safety awareness. Tapping a helpline chip simply opens your device's default phone dialer; the app never places automatic calls or accesses your call logs.

4. Data Security, Children's Privacy & Data Deletion

  • No Sale of Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to advertisers or data brokers.
  • Children & Students: The app is educational and suitable for students and general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
  • Clear Your Data Anytime: You can erase all locally stored preferences and cached data at any time by tapping Menu (☰) → Clear Cache inside the app or clearing app storage in your Android device settings.
